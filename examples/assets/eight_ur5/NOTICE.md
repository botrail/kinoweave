# Eight-UR5 demonstration assets

Robot geometry derives from the pinned example-robot-data UR5 assets described
in [the original attribution](../ur5/NOTICE.md). The accompanying hash-checked
`notices/` files preserve their original license and attribution text.

These GLBs preserve the source robot mesh detail, coordinates and native link
bindings. The complete native collision-sphere model remains the planning
geometry. The torch and workpiece are generated demonstration geometry.
Their dimensions and nominal gap do not qualify welding equipment or a process.
